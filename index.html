About Me

I'm a master's student in Computer Science at Brown University, where I work in the Humans to Robots Laboratory (H2R) under the guidance of Professor Stefanie Tellex. My research interests include robot learning, human-in-the-loop learning, and egocentric perception. I'm passionate about making robot learning more data-efficient and developing systems that are easier to train, adapt, and deploy.
