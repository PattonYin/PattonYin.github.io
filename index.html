Learning from people.

I'm Jiyang (Patton) Yin, a master's student in Computer Science at Brown University. I work in the Humans to Robots Laboratory (H2R), advised by Professor Stefanie Tellex.

My research spans robot learning, human-in-the-loop learning, and egocentric perception. I'm interested in making robot learning more data-efficient and developing systems that are easier to train, adapt, and deploy.
